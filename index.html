Our Story

A culinary
journey like
no other

Born from a passion for transforming the finest seasonal ingredients into memorable experiences, AURUM has been Casablanca's crown jewel of fine dining for over seventeen years.

Our culinary philosophy centers on the marriage of classical French technique with the bold, aromatic soul of Moroccan cuisine — creating a dialogue between two great culinary traditions.

— Chef Karim Mansour
Farm to Table Only locally sourced, seasonal ingredients from trusted producers
Sommelier Curated wine pairings from France, Italy & Morocco
Private Dining Exclusive rooms for intimate events and celebrations
Live Music Jazz evenings every Friday and Saturday night
